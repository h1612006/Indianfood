Terms & Conditions

Ordering

Prices are shown in euros and include the items you choose. Delivery fees and minimum order amounts are shown in your basket before you place the order. The total is always calculated by our server.

Payment

You can choose cash, card or Bizum. Cash and Bizum are paid when you collect or receive your order. If online card payment is available, it is handled by our payment provider and we never see your card number.

Allergies and spice

Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy, mustard and sesame. Tell us about any allergy in the notes when you order. Please choose your spice level carefully: High and Super High are very hot.

Changes and cancellations

Ask the shop as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. Once cooking has started we may not be able to cancel it.

Your data

We keep your name, email, phone number, address, orders and comments only to prepare and deliver your orders and to improve the shop. Ask us if you want your account removed.

Comments

Comments you send are read by the shop owner. Please keep them respectful.
